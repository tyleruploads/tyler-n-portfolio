About Tyler N

I am Tyler N., a self-taught developer from Maryland passionate about Web and App development. I thrive on solving problems with specific, concrete methods, clean code, and explaining things to the public with good READMEs and effective social media.

My Background Story

When I was much younger, I used to "code" a bit, while really just trying to have AI create entire applications for me. But, in the sixth grade, I began to learn to code on my own, and I caught on very quickly. I realized that when I was younger, I was missing out on the best part of coding: the learning.

I now treat AI as a quick reference tool or debugging aid, and believe that the best way to make something is to make it with your own intellect. To pick up coding quickly, I followed through with the Python Crash Course book by No Starch Press, treating each chapter like a unit at school where you need to demonstrate you actually understand each section. For every other coding language, I learned by doing. I found basic examples of things in that language, researched how to do specific things, then just did them.
